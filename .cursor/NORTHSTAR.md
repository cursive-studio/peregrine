# Peregrine Creative Group — North Star

## Vision

Peregrine Creative Group is not a collection of disconnected businesses.

It is a modern distribution and manufacturing group operating across products, apparel, packaging, and consumer goods — built around speed, adaptability, and scalable supply.

The website should communicate this clearly.

Peregrine is the parent system.
Its divisions are expressions of the same operational mindset:
- agile manufacturing
- rapid fulfilment
- product customisation
- scalable distribution
- modern supply relationships

The website must unify these capabilities into one coherent brand story.

---

# Core Positioning

## What Peregrine Is

Peregrine Creative Group is a multi-sector product and distribution company operating across:
- reusable drinkware
- custom event products
- garment manufacturing and fulfilment
- food distribution
- branded merchandise
- scalable sourcing

The business operates as both:
- a supplier
- a manufacturing partner
- a distribution network
- a creative production partner

The website should position Peregrine as:
> a modern infrastructure company for physical products.

Not simply:
- a merchandise supplier
- a print company
- an ecommerce catalogue
- a holding company

The experience should feel:
- operational
- capable
- global
- adaptable
- fast-moving
- trustworthy

---

# Core Website Goal

The website exists to answer three questions quickly:

1. Who is Peregrine?
2. What capabilities does the group provide?
3. Why should someone work with them?

Everything else is secondary.

The website should:
- establish trust
- communicate scale
- show operational flexibility
- explain the ecosystem of brands/divisions
- create clear entry points into services

The site is not primarily an ecommerce experience.

It is:
- a brand platform
- a capability showcase
- a lead generation tool
- a business credibility layer

# Visual System

## Typography

Typography should feel:
- structured
- industrial
- premium
- readable

Avoid:
- overly expressive display fonts
- trendy tech aesthetics

Good direction:
- Helvetica Now
- Suisse
- Inter Tight
- Neue Haas Grotesk

---

## Colour

Base palette should feel:
- neutral
- industrial
- flexible

Suggested approach:
- black
- white
- warm greys
- muted industrial tones

Each division may have:
- a subtle accent colour
- its own photographic atmosphere

But the system must remain unified.

# Motion Principles

Motion should communicate:
- systems
- logistics
- movement
- flow
- infrastructure

Avoid:
- excessive cinematic effects
- heavy scroll hijacking
- novelty interactions

Preferred motion:
- smooth transitions
- layered reveals
- modular movement
- subtle parallax
- product choreography

# UX Principles

## Clarity > Decoration

## Fast Perceived Load
